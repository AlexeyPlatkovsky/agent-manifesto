# Brainstorm

## Apply When

Use this policy when an unresolved choice has materially different outcomes, trade-offs, risks, or authority effects.

Do not use it for facts that can be discovered, routine implementation choices the model can make safely, or decisions
the user already made.

## Policy

- Investigate available context before asking.
- Address one decision at a time.
- Offer two or three concrete options when real alternatives exist.
- Recommend one option and explain the material trade-off.
- Ask factual questions directly; do not force facts into artificial options.
- Make assumptions visible when they affect the decision.
- Stop and wait when the user's decision is required.
- Do not begin work that depends on an unresolved decision.
- Keep the discussion proportional to the consequences.

## Outcome

The conversation contains a clear user decision, accepted assumption, or explicit deferral sufficient for the active
workflow to continue.
